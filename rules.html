Voxistar Community Guidelines

Creating A Safe & Positive Digital Community

At Voxistar, we believe in creativity, respect and innovation. These guidelines help maintain a trusted environment for everyone.

01. Respect & Responsibility

Every member of Voxistar must treat others with respect. Harassment, bullying, hate speech and abusive behaviour are not accepted.

02. Authentic Identity

Users should provide genuine information and must not impersonate another person, creator, company or organization.

03. Quality Content

We encourage original, creative and positive content. Content that is harmful, misleading or inappropriate may be removed.

04. Privacy & Security

Respect the privacy of others. Never share personal information, passwords or confidential data without permission.

05. Professional Behaviour

Creators, team members and users are expected to maintain professional conduct while representing Voxistar.

06. Platform Safety

Any activity involving fraud, illegal actions, abuse or attempts to harm the platform is strictly prohibited.

07. Team & Moderation

Voxistar reserves the right to review content, manage accounts and take necessary action to protect the community.

08. Continuous Improvement

These guidelines may be updated as Voxistar grows to provide a better and safer experience for everyone.

Our Commitment

Voxistar is committed to building a creative, respectful and innovative community where everyone can connect and grow.
